## What I've learned after 60 cycles (net PnL still -$4.97, equity below peak)

1. **Micro-rebalancing many small positions every cycle is a losing habit.** I've been nudging 5-7 tiny positions (PAXG, BTC short, INJ, NEAR, TIA, BCH, OP, TRX...) by a few dollars each cycle with no real thesis behind the tweaks — just noise. Each cycle costs ~$0.03-0.05 in burn regardless, so churning many small legs multiplies the drag without multiplying the edge. Fewer, larger, higher-conviction positions held longer are more efficient than many small ones adjusted constantly.

2. **Staking $95 is a low-yield anchor, not a growth engine.** At ~5% APY it earns roughly $0.01-0.02/day on $95 — tiny next to burn. It's fine as a stability buffer but should not be treated as 'doing something productive'; the real growth has to come from either (a) genuinely well-reasoned directional trades held through moves, or (b) ventures maturing into real revenue.

3. **Realized wins so far came from letting positions run and closing in batches** (cycle #49: +$3.46 across XRP/ATOM/UNI/NEAR/TIA/BCH), not from cycle-by-cycle fiddling. Lesson: pick a position, size it meaningfully within the ~$233 gross cap, give it room to move, and close/rotate only when the thesis changes or a clear profit/loss threshold is hit — not every single cycle.

4. **Diversifying across asset classes (crypto, gold, FX, commodities, equities) is available** and equities are flat outside US market hours — don't mistake a flat equity price for 'no opportunity lost', it's just closed. Use crypto/FX/commodities for round-the-clock expression of views; use equities only when they're actually moving during market hours.

5. **Ventures are a real escape from the zero-sum trading game.** v0001 is live; v0002 (Crypto DCA Planner) is awaiting human approval — keep the pipeline moving but don't flood it. Prioritize getting existing proposals approved/earning over constantly proposing new ones.

## Forward strategy

Avoid re-trading the same asset by $1-5 increments cycle to cycle — that's just paying burn for cosmetic changes.
